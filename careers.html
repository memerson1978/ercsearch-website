Our vision

ERC Search is a highly collaborative firm dedicated to providing a highly personal service. We continually track, network and partner with the very best strategic and commercial talent to provide insight and help to decipher options. Where appropriate we provide qualified introductions to some of the most exciting high-growth PE/VC backed businesses as well as select corporates and market leading boutique consulting firms.

Equally we work closely with clients to ensure candidates presented are well suited to the culture, values and goals of their companies and we aim to ensure that no stone is left unturned. We have a deep vested interest in the long-term mission of our clients businesses and helping them to build world class, high performance teams. We highly value the network that we have forged through building trust, upholding the highest levels of integrity combined with good old fashioned hard work!

Central to our ethos lies honesty, frankness and integrity. This should lead to the best long term interests and outcomes for all parties.

Considering a career at ERC Search?

Long days, rich in stress and poor in reward. Sound familiar? If you work in a traditional recruitment firm it probably does. For those firms stuck in the past it means their recruiters spending their time in an environment clouded by office politics and working independently within an organisation "running a desk" rather than embracing more open and collaborative ways of working.

Every day we network with and assist exceptional and passionate people with the energy and ambition to succeed - and it’s our role to help them release the full potential of their skills and experience with a highly personal service combined with sound and informed advice.

Our vision is to be the leading and most trusted partner for top-tier strategic and management consulting talent by leveraging our well established network of exceptional individuals and alumni combined with our own internal capabilities and passion to assist elite global talent and leading organisations; be it large or small.
